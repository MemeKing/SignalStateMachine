General-purpose "finite state machine" using attachable nodes and signals. Suitable for 2D and 3D player controllers, enemy AI, guns, or whatever else.

There are quite a few other fsm addons, many of them using the same "states are nodes" design as this one. However I've found the others too complex and rigid, limiting what can be done.
